ARTICLE 1 | AGREEMENT AND GENERAL PROVISIONS

1.1 Integrated Design-Build Authorization. Owner engages Design-Builder to provide the design, preconstruction, procurement, construction, commissioning, and closeout services included in the Contract Documents. Design and construction are authorized by this Agreement as one integrated undertaking. Construction may begin after the stated commencement conditions are satisfied without another agreement between Owner and Design-Builder.

1.2 Contract Documents. The Contract Documents consist of this Agreement, the specifically listed Owner's Criteria and basis documents, listed attachments, approved Change Orders, written directives issued under Article 7, and other documents expressly incorporated by a writing signed by both parties. They form the entire agreement and supersede prior negotiations and representations concerning the same Work.

1.3 Definitions. Work means the included design, preconstruction, procurement, construction, commissioning, and closeout services. Project means the total undertaking of which the Work may be all or a part. Contract Sum means the fixed lump sum stated in Article 2, as adjusted only by an authorized Modification. Owner's Criteria means Owner's program, performance, quality, site, schedule, operational, and other requirements stated or incorporated in Article 4. Design Professional means a properly licensed architect, engineer, or other professional. Instruments of Service means drawings, models, specifications, studies, calculations, and similar professional work product. Day means calendar day unless expressly stated otherwise.

1.4 Project Team. Each party shall act through its authorized representative. A representative may bind the appointing party only within the authority stated in this Agreement or separately confirmed in writing. A representative shall not be changed without reasonable written notice.

ARTICLE 2 | CONTRACT SUM AND SCOPE OF THE WORK

2.1 Lump Sum Contract Sum and Upfront Payment. Owner shall pay Design-Builder the Contract Sum stated below in current funds for complete performance of the Work. The Contract Sum includes design and professional services, preconstruction, labor, materials, equipment, supervision, general conditions, overhead and profit, and the other components identified in the Contract Documents. The Initial Payment Due Upon Execution is an advance against the Contract Sum and a condition precedent to Design-Builder beginning design, preconstruction, procurement, or mobilization. It is applied to the earliest applications for payment, is earned as services are performed or commitments are incurred, and any unapplied unearned balance is credited at Final Payment or termination. Delay in receiving the upfront payment extends the Contract Time. The Contract Sum may be adjusted only by a Modification authorized under Article 7.
