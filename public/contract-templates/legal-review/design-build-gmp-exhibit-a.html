A.1.7 Budget and GMP Are Different. Owner's approved Project budget is an Owner planning target and may include land, financing, Owner vendors, furniture, equipment, reserves, taxes, and other costs outside this Contract. The GMP is the maximum Contract Sum for the Work defined by the listed pricing documents, subject to authorized adjustment. Neither amount changes the other, and no budget line not included in the GMP becomes Design-Builder's responsibility.

A.1.8 Payment Applications. Design-Builder shall submit applications monthly using the cutoff below. Each application shall show the current schedule of values, percentage complete, eligible stored materials, Cost of the Work, Fee, approved Changes, allowance and contingency activity, retainage, prior payments, and amount due, together with the Project-specific support identified in A.5.10.

A.1.9 Strict Payment Timing. Within seven calendar days after receipt of a complete application, Owner shall approve it or provide a written notice identifying each disputed line, amount, and basis. Owner shall pay all undisputed portions within fifteen calendar days after receipt. A dispute, lender review, or certification issue affecting one item does not delay other amounts. Overdue undisputed amounts bear interest at twelve percent per year or the highest lawful rate, whichever is lower. Design-Builder will give any additional notice required by mandatory law.

A.1.12 Stored Materials and Advance Procurement. Eligible stored materials may be billed when identified to the Project, protected, insured, and supported by reasonable evidence of purchase and title. Off-site storage requires reasonable advance approval of location and controls. Advance deposits and procurement commitments necessary to preserve price, schedule, fabrication slot, or availability are payable when documented and included in the GMP basis.

A.1.13 Final Payment. Final payment is due within fifteen calendar days after Final Completion, final application, final accounting, required closeout documents, lien documentation, and consent of surety if applicable. Owner may withhold only a reasonable amount tied to a specifically identified incomplete or disputed item and shall pay the balance. An audit does not postpone undisputed final payment.

A.1.14 Pay-if-Paid Owner Acknowledgment. Owner acknowledges that Design-Builder's lower-tier agreements may make Design-Builder's actual receipt of corresponding Owner payment an express condition precedent to lower-tier payment for the same Work. To the fullest extent allowed by law, Design-Builder has no duty to finance Owner's Project or advance unpaid Owner funds, except to the extent Design-Builder caused nonpayment through its material breach concerning that Work. Owner nonpayment may lead to lower-tier nonpayment, notices, liens, loss of labor or material availability, and suspension. Except to the extent caused by Design-Builder, Owner bears the resulting cost and time, including escalation, storage, shutdown, extended general conditions, demobilization, remobilization, supported lower-tier claims, lien-resolution expense, and applicable Fee. Design-Builder shall pay corresponding undisputed lower-tier amounts after receipt as required by the applicable agreement and mandatory law.

A.1.15 Allowances. Each allowance is limited to the purpose and included cost components shown in A.3.3. Owner shall make selections by the required decision date. When actual committed cost is known, Design-Builder shall present the comparison and the parties shall reconcile the allowance by Change Order. A schedule-of-values transfer or invoice does not replace the Change Order.

A.1.16 Contingencies. Design-Builder contingency is available for reimbursable costs within the original scope that were not specifically identified but are reasonably necessary to complete that scope, including estimating development, coordination gaps, minor buyout variance, and ordinary construction uncertainty. It is not a guaranteed credit and is not available for Owner-directed additions. Owner contingency, if listed, is controlled by Owner and is not part of the GMP unless the summary expressly includes it. Contingency use will be reported in the monthly cost report.

A.1.17 Savings and Final GMP Reconciliation. At final reconciliation, Savings means the positive difference between the adjusted GMP and the final reimbursable Cost of the Work plus earned Fee and other Contract amounts, after all Changes, known Claims, closeout cost, allowances, contingencies, and commitments are resolved. Savings are allocated below. Savings do not include deleted-scope amounts that preserve Fee, design cost, general conditions, overhead, or other costs under the Agreement.
