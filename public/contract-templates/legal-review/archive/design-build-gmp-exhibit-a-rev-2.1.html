ARTICLE A.5 | COST OF THE WORK

A.5.1 General. Cost of the Work means the reasonable, necessary, Project-specific costs actually incurred by Design-Builder in proper performance of the Work and within the categories below. Cost of the Work excludes Design-Builder's Fee except where expressly stated and remains subject to the adjusted GMP.

A.5.2 Labor Costs. Cost of the Work includes wages of construction workers directly employed on the Work; approved supervisory and administrative personnel stationed at the site; authorized off-site personnel directly engaged in expediting or Project production; and payroll taxes, insurance, assessments, and customary benefits attributable to those wages. Home-office personnel are included only if expressly identified below.

 {{LABOR_BURDEN_AND_OVERTIME_TREATMENT}}



A.5.3 Design, Consultant, Subcontract, and Supplier Costs. Cost of the Work includes payments properly made to Design Professionals, consultants, subcontractors, and suppliers under written agreements for the Work, including authorized change, cancellation, and closeout cost.

A.5.4 Materials and Equipment. Cost of the Work includes purchase, transportation, storage, and protection of materials and equipment incorporated or intended for incorporation, together with reasonable waste. Unused excess materials paid by Owner become Owner's property or may be sold with net proceeds credited to the Cost of the Work.

A.5.5 Temporary Facilities and Equipment. Cost of the Work includes transportation, installation, maintenance, operation, rental, minor repair, dismantling, and removal of Project-specific temporary facilities, machinery, equipment, tools, safety systems, site offices, utilities, and communications. Charges for Design-Builder-owned equipment shall not exceed commercially reasonable rental value and any agreed rate schedule.

A.5.6 Project Administration and Closeout. Cost of the Work includes Project-specific document reproduction, electronic project systems, postage, delivery, photographs, cleanup, debris disposal, security, winter conditions, temporary protection, commissioning support, closeout materials, and other field administration identified in the schedule of values.

A.5.7 Insurance, Bonds, Taxes, Permits, and Testing. Cost of the Work includes Project-attributable insurance and bond premiums; sales, use, and similar taxes; permits, licenses, inspections, and utility charges assigned to Design-Builder; required laboratory testing and special inspections not caused by nonconforming Work; and royalties or license fees for required products and processes.

A.5.8 Other Approved Costs and Emergencies. Cost of the Work includes other costs approved in advance in writing and reasonable cost incurred in an emergency to prevent threatened injury, damage, or loss. Cost of correcting damaged or nonconforming Work is included only to the extent the condition was not caused by Design-Builder's or a lower-tier party's negligence or breach and the cost is not recovered from insurance, surety, warranty, or another responsible party.

A.5.9 Related-Party Transactions. Before incurring a reimbursable cost with an affiliate or other related party, Design-Builder shall disclose the relationship, scope, and anticipated price. The cost is reimbursable only if commercially reasonable and approved under the project-specific rule below.

RELATED-PARTY TRANSACTION

AGREED TERM

Related-party approval and pricing support

A.5.10 Costs Not Reimbursable. Cost of the Work excludes principal-office salaries and expenses not expressly included; general overhead compensated by the Fee; capital expenses and financing charges; fines caused by Design-Builder's violation; cost caused by Design-Builder's or a lower-tier party's negligence or material breach; cost recovered from insurance, surety, warranty, or a responsible third party; cost not described as reimbursable; and cost above the adjusted GMP that is not otherwise compensable.

A.5.11 Discounts, Rebates, Refunds, and Surplus. Trade discounts, rebates, refunds, insurance proceeds, and net proceeds from sale of surplus materials attributable to costs paid by Owner shall be credited to the Cost of the Work. Cash discounts accrue to the party whose funds enabled the discount.

A.5.12 Procurement and Owner-Directed Awards. Design-Builder shall procure qualified Design Professionals, consultants, subcontractors, and suppliers on commercially reasonable terms. If Owner directs use of another bidder instead of Design-Builder's recommended qualified conforming bidder, the GMP and Contract Time shall be adjusted for the resulting difference.

A.5.13 Accounting Records and Audit. Design-Builder shall maintain detailed records sufficient to substantiate Cost of the Work and shall preserve them for the period stated below. Owner and its auditor may review relevant records during regular business hours on reasonable notice. Review is limited to this Project and is subject to privilege, cybersecurity, confidentiality, and protection of unrelated business information.
