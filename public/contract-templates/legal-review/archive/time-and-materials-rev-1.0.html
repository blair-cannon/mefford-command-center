CONTRACT DOCUMENTS AND EXECUTION

By executing this Agreement, Owner authorizes Contractor to proceed with the Work before a total Project budget or final price is established. Owner shall pay for labor at the agreed billing rates, materials, equipment, subcontractors, consultants, and other reimbursable costs, plus the agreed Contractor Fee / Profit Percentage and any specifically stated markups. No estimate, forecast, target, purchase authorization, or progress report creates a maximum price unless both parties sign a written Modification that expressly states a not-to-exceed amount.

ARTICLE 1 | AGREEMENT AND GENERAL PROVISIONS

1.1 Time-and-Materials Engagement. Owner engages Contractor to furnish the supervision, labor, materials, equipment, temporary facilities, procurement, coordination, construction, testing support, commissioning support, and closeout included in the Work. Compensation is determined under Article 3 rather than by a fixed total price.

1.2 Contract Documents. The Contract Documents consist of this Agreement, specifically listed Work authorizations, drawings, specifications, reports, attachments, approved Modifications, written directives issued under Article 9, and other documents expressly incorporated by a writing signed by both parties. They form the entire agreement and supersede prior negotiations and representations concerning the same Work.

1.3 Definitions. Work means the construction and related services authorized under this Agreement. Project means the total undertaking of which the Work may be all or a part. T&M Cost means the labor charges, materials, equipment, subcontractors, consultants, permits, freight, taxes, and other reimbursable items calculated under Article 3. Contractor Fee / Profit means the agreed percentage added under Article 3. Modification means a written amendment, Change Order, or written directive authorized under Article 9. Day means calendar day unless expressly stated otherwise.
