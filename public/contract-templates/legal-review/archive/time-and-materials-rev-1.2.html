CONTRACT DOCUMENTS AND EXECUTION

By executing this Agreement, Owner authorizes Contractor to proceed before a total Project budget or final price is established. Owner shall pay labor at the agreed billing rates, reimbursable T&M Cost, any expressly stated item-specific markups, and the Contractor Fee / Profit Percentage calculated under Article 3. Cost-to-date reports, funding information, anticipated commitments, and planning estimates are not a maximum price, warranty, or prediction of final cost unless both parties sign a Modification that expressly creates a not-to-exceed amount.

ARTICLE 1 | AGREEMENT AND GENERAL PROVISIONS

1.1 Time-and-Materials Engagement. Owner engages Contractor to furnish the supervision, labor, materials, equipment, temporary facilities, procurement, coordination, construction, testing support, commissioning support, and closeout included in the Work. Compensation is determined under Article 3 rather than by a fixed total price.

1.2 Contract Documents. The Contract Documents consist of this Agreement, specifically listed Work authorizations, drawings, specifications, reports, attachments, approved Modifications, written directives issued under Article 9, and other documents expressly incorporated by a writing signed by both parties. They form the entire agreement and supersede prior negotiations and representations concerning the same Work.

1.3 Definitions. Work means the construction and related services authorized under this Agreement. Project means the total undertaking of which the Work may be all or a part. T&M Cost means labor charges at the stated billing rates and the reimbursable material, equipment, subcontractor, consultant, permit, freight, tax, and other direct costs described in Article 3, before any Item-Specific Markup or Contractor Fee / Profit. Actual Net Cost means the amount actually paid or payable after ordinary trade discounts, rebates, refunds, and credits, plus Project-specific tax, freight, delivery, storage, and handling permitted by Article 3. Item-Specific Markup means only the percentage, if any, entered for a listed cost category. Contractor Fee / Profit means the overall percentage stated in Article 3 and applied once to T&M Cost. Neither an Item-Specific Markup nor Contractor Fee / Profit is applied to the other unless a signed Work Authorization expressly states otherwise. Modification means a written amendment, Change Order, or Work Authorization signed by both parties. Day means calendar day unless expressly stated otherwise.

1.4 Project Team. Each party shall act through its authorized representative. A representative may bind the appointing party only within the authority stated in this Agreement or separately confirmed in writing.
