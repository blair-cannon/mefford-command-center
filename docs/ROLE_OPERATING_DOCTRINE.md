# Mefford Role Operating Doctrine

## Governing outcome

Every Main Command Center screen, record, dashboard, alert, escalation, and accounting exception must help an accountable person produce a safe, on-schedule, profitable result. A module is not complete merely because it stores data. It must expose the next decision, the business consequence, the person who owns recovery, the role that assists, and the deadline.

## Role contract

| Role | Non-negotiable mission | Live measures | Cross-role handoff | Ownership trigger |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Site Superintendent | Build safely and keep field production on the current schedule. | Current Daily Log; open safety; late activities; open quality; tomorrow plan | Field truth to PM | Unresolved safety, unrecovered critical path, repeated missing field visibility |
| Project Manager | Hit the contract schedule and protect or improve forecast profit. | Baseline and completion; current ETC/EAC; forecast profit and margin; changes; buyout; billing | Accounting truth and Superintendent production | Projected loss, material margin erosion, final-completion risk, uncontrolled cost exposure |
| Estimator | Deliver complete, competitive, defensible proposals by the promised deadline. | Due in seven days; overdue; unassigned; ready for review; bid coverage | Sales scope and close strategy | Overdue qualified proposal, material scope gap, unreviewed pricing |
| Sales | Maintain qualified pipeline large enough to hit the goal and convert it into profitable work. | Awarded pace; open and weighted pipeline; goal coverage; next moves due | Estimating capacity and proposal quality | Weighted gap to goal, material opportunity without movement, close pace risk |
| Accounting | Give PMs current, reconciled cost and cash truth early enough to change the outcome. | Current WIP packs; profit signals; cost exceptions; AP/AR; billing and receipts | Decision-ready exception to PM | Unreconciled project loss, stale decision data, cost/cash exception threatening action |
| Company Leadership | Remove barriers and enforce accountability only where operating risk requires it. | Protected red flags; accountable role; recovery action; deadline | Barrier removal and permanent decision | Safety, projected loss, contract completion, persistent role inaction |

## Alert contract

Every operational signal must carry:

1. Accountable role and named person when available.
2. Operating outcome at risk.
3. Business consequence in plain language.
4. Required action and governing live link.
5. Supporting role and its exact contribution.
6. Required date.
7. Ownership visibility reason.

## Profit protection

- The standing forecast gross-margin floor is 15%.
- A forecast at or below zero gross profit forces Project Health Red and creates immediate PM, Accounting, and ownership work.
- Accounting reconciles the source data; the PM owns ETC, EAC, risks, and the recovery decision.
- Project-loss, unresolved safety, and overdue final completion are protected controls. They cannot be disabled or suspended by a project exception.

## Escalation

- At due time: direct reminder to the accountable person.
- 48 hours overdue: direct critical escalation.
- 72 hours overdue: role manager or the project's PM receives the blocker, business consequence, and recovery expectation.
- 96 hours overdue: Company Owner receives an Ownership Red Flag naming the accountable role, consequence, recovery action, and deadline.

## Release reconciliation

The release gate verifies the shared doctrine, role scorecards, profit-loss Project Health guardrail, PM decision-support accounting queue, My Work role/outcome decoration, role-aware escalations, and User Guide language. Main is the only application affected by this doctrine release; the Pilot and Dashboard Display are separate applications.
