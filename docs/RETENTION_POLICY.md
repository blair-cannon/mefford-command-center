# Retention and Legal Hold Policy

| Record class | Minimum retention |
| --- | --- |
| Contracts, amendments, change orders, lien waivers | Project completion + 10 years |
| Ledger, journals, invoices, receipts, billing, payroll reports | 7 years |
| Safety incidents, training, inspections | Longer of 10 years or applicable law |
| Personnel and benefits | Employment end + 7 years, subject to law |
| Drawings, RFIs, submittals, schedules, logs, closeout | Project completion + 10 years |
| Access, security, scheduler, delivery, and audit evidence | 7 years |
| Superseded governed templates | While any dependent record is retained |

These are conservative company minimums, not legal advice. Legal may extend them. A legal hold suspends all deletion for matching custodians, projects, companies, dates, record classes, storage keys, and audits. Release requires written Legal authorization. Retention expiration never authorizes deletion; use the controlled quarantine and irreversible-purge workflow.
