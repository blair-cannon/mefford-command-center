# Work screen requirements

Internal work screens must lead with records, fields, filters, and actions. Keep headers and toolbars compact.

Do not add instructional or FYI panels, process diagrams, role missions or promises, template statistics, source-count summaries, or promotional descriptions to work screens. This includes Estimating, Sales, Proposal Studio, Contract Studios, Accounting, project work, and administration. My Work / My Home is the exception where narrative content is allowed.

Preserve field labels, live business metrics, actionable errors, unsaved-change notices, permission and approval controls, and signature consent. Keep instructions needed to understand a specific consequential input brief and next to that input. Document previews and issued documents retain their actual content, branding, certifications, and signatures.

Remove unused layout columns and oversized framing when removing content. Do not enlarge the compact sidebar. Preserve mobile and touch usability.

Work screens must fit their available width with vertical scrolling only. Wrap navigation and toolbars, reflow dense tables into labeled records, and stack panels as space narrows. Do not hide overflowing data, shrink the whole application, or create horizontal scroll areas. Check narrow containers as well as narrow viewports. Sales funnel cards show only project, customer, commercial value, and salesperson; preserve compact spacing for ten projects per stage. Use the proposal value from proposal onward and the recorded contract value for awarded work.

Run the existing verified release build before publishing; do not skip workflow, pressure-test, coverage, or mutation gates. Presentation tests must check the current controls and absence of clutter without restoring obsolete banners or weakening business rules.
